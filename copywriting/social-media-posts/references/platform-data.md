# Platform Data Reference — LinkedIn & X

Last updated: April 2026
Sources: Buffer 2026 State of Social Media Engagement (45M+ posts analyzed), LinkedIn algorithm studies, Postiv.ai, AuthoredUp

## X (Twitter) — Engagement by Format

| Format | Median Engagement Rate | Notes |
|--------|----------------------|-------|
| Text-only | 3.56% | Leads all formats |
| Image | 3.40% | Gap is razor-thin; high breakout potential |
| Video | 2.96% | Consistent but lower ceiling |
| Link posts | 2.26% | Worst performer — avoid links in posts |

**Key behaviors:**
- X is fundamentally a text-first, microblogging platform
- Hot takes, real-time reactions, punchy one-liners perform best
- Early engagement in first hour signals algorithm to expand reach
- Hashtags have minimal algorithmic benefit on X — use 0–1 max
- Threads (4–8 posts) work well for depth without losing reach
- Native media (uploaded directly) gets up to 40% more engagement than linked media

**Ideal post length:** 70–100 characters for quick reads

## LinkedIn — Engagement by Format

| Format | Avg Engagement Rate | vs Text-only |
|--------|--------------------|-|
| Carousel (PDF) | 24.42% | +585% |
| Multi-image | ~15% | +300% |
| Text + single image | ~8–12% | +150–200% |
| Video (short, <60s) | ~6% | +100% |
| Text-only | 3.0–4.0% | Baseline |
| Polls | 4.40% | Good for research, not authority |
| External links | Penalized | Avoid in post body |

**Key behaviors:**
- Algorithm prioritizes dwell time — carousels win because swiping counts as engagement
- First 140 characters visible on desktop before "See more" (110 on mobile)
- 60%+ of LinkedIn users are on mobile — short paragraphs are non-negotiable
- Posts with faces get 38% more likes than stock images or graphics
- External links in post body reduce reach — put in first comment instead
- Early comments in first hour dramatically increase algorithmic distribution
- LinkedIn's "Brew 360" algorithm update increased content half-life — old posts resurface more
- Consistency matters more than frequency — algorithm learns your patterns
- Video usage up 69% on LinkedIn but text+image still delivers best ROI for effort

**Optimal posting times:** Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am and 12–2pm (audience timezone)

## Algorithm Signals That Matter (Both Platforms)

**Positive signals:**
- Comments (weighted highest)
- Saves/bookmarks
- Shares/reposts
- Dwell time (how long people spend reading)
- Replies to your replies (shows conversation depth)

**Negative signals (LinkedIn):**
- External links (suppresses reach)
- Low engagement in first hour
- Excessive hashtags (5+ looks spammy)
- Posting and ghosting (not replying to comments)

**Neutral or context-dependent:**
- Likes (lowest weight of all engagement types)
- Hashtags on X (minimal effect either way)

## Format Specs

### X
- Character limit: 280 (standard), up to 25,000 (X Premium)
- Image: 1600×900px (16:9) optimal
- Video: Under 60 seconds for best completion rate
- Thread: 4–8 posts optimal

### LinkedIn
- Post character limit: 3,000 characters
- Hook visibility: 140 chars desktop / 110 chars mobile
- Carousel: Upload as PDF, up to 300 pages (keep under 20 slides)
- Image: 1200×627px (horizontal) or 1080×1080px (square)
- Video: 30–90 seconds sweet spot; always add captions (85% watch with sound off)
