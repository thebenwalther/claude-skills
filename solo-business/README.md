# Solo Business Skills

Frameworks from Alex Hormozi, Dan Koe, Justin Welsh, Dickie Bush, Nicolas Cole, and Pat Flynn for solo online business operators. These skills cover the full arc of building a one-person business — from finding your niche to validating ideas, designing offers, building a product ladder, and creating a content engine.

7 skills, designed to be used in sequence as your business matures.

---

## The Recommended Order

If you're starting from scratch, work through these in order:

1. **niche-clarity-engine** — find what you should build around
2. **solo-biz-validator** — confirm people will pay for it before you build
3. **solo-biz-phase-diagnostic** — diagnose where you are and what to work on next
4. **grand-slam-offer-architect** — make the offer irresistible
5. **product-ladder-designer** — build a full revenue ecosystem
6. **content-flywheel-builder** — create a sustainable content engine
7. **category-creation-workshop** — own your space when competition increases

---

## Skills

### niche-clarity-engine
Runs three alignment frameworks in sequence — the **Airport Test** (future vision), the **History Test** (past pattern recognition), and the **Anti-Vision** (what you don't want) — to identify a niche that fits your life, skills, and values.

**Triggers:** `help me find my niche`, `niche clarity`, `what should my business be about`, `airport test`, `anti-vision`, `history test`, `I don't know what to focus on`, `what's my unfair advantage`, `I have too many interests`, `I keep pivoting`

---

### solo-biz-validator
Validates a business idea using the **P.L.A.N. framework** (Problems, Language, Anecdotes, Needs) and a structured pre-sale test sequence. Kills bad ideas fast and sharpens good ones before you build anything.

**Triggers:** `validate my idea`, `will this work`, `is this a good business idea`, `P.L.A.N. framework`, `should I build this`, `test my business idea`, `pre-sale test`, `will anyone buy this`, `I think people would want this`

---

### solo-biz-phase-diagnostic
Diagnoses which of the **six phases** of building a solo online business you're currently in — and tells you exactly what framework to apply next. Prevents the most common solopreneur mistake: working on the wrong thing at the wrong time.

**Triggers:** `what phase am I in`, `what should I focus on`, `solo biz diagnostic`, `phase diagnostic`, `I feel stuck in my business`, `where should I focus next`, `I'm overwhelmed`, `I don't know what to prioritize`

---

### grand-slam-offer-architect
Builds an irresistible offer using **Alex Hormozi's Grand Slam Offer** framework — the Value Equation, bonus stacking, scarcity and urgency mechanics, and guarantee design.

**Triggers:** `grand slam offer`, `build my offer`, `hormozi offer`, `value equation`, `make my offer irresistible`, `offer architecture`, `nobody is buying my thing`, `how do I price this`, `my offer sucks`, `I don't know what to charge`, `people say it's too expensive`

---

### product-ladder-designer
Designs a full product ladder — from free lead magnets to high-ticket services — that captures value at every stage of the customer journey. Based on **Dan Koe's Product Ladder** and **Justin Welsh's Creator Revenue Stack**.

**Triggers:** `product ladder`, `revenue stack`, `what products should I create`, `product funnel`, `how do I monetize`, `build my product suite`, `I only have one product`, `upsell strategy`, `I need more revenue streams`, `what should I sell next`

---

### content-flywheel-builder
Builds a complete content engine: a **Content Matrix** (themes × formats), a **Rule of One** 90-day launch plan, and a **5-12-3 repurposing strategy** across platforms. Output is a ready-to-execute system, not a list of ideas.

**Triggers:** `content flywheel`, `content matrix`, `content system`, `rule of one`, `5-12-3`, `help me plan my content`, `I don't know what to post`, `build my content engine`, `content strategy`, `repurposing strategy`, `I keep starting and stopping with content`

---

### category-creation-workshop
Helps you identify or invent a category you can own — a **superniche** with proprietary language, unique positioning, and differentiation that makes direct competition irrelevant. Based on **Dickie Bush and Nicolas Cole's** category creation methodology.

**Triggers:** `category creation`, `create a category`, `superniche`, `how do I stand out`, `I'm competing with everyone`, `differentiation`, `category design`, `own my niche`, `category of one`, `there are too many people doing what I do`, `how do I stop being a commodity`
