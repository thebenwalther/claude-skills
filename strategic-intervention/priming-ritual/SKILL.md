# Priming Ritual Builder

**How you start your morning shapes your entire day.**

Most people wake up reactive — checking phones, scrolling, letting the world's agenda hijack their state before they've even gotten out of bed. Priming flips this: you deliberately create a peak state FIRST, then engage with the day from strength.

Tony Robbins does his priming practice every day, even when traveling. Why? Because state is that important.

---

## The Components of Priming

A complete priming ritual has 5 elements:

### 1. Breath Work
**Energize the body through breath.** Breath is the fastest way to change your physiology and biochemistry.

Options:
- **Kapalbhati (Breath of Fire):** 30 rapid exhales through the nose, forceful exhale, passive inhale
- **3-Part Breath:** Inhale to belly, then ribs, then chest. Exhale reverse order. Slow, deliberate.
- **Box Breathing:** 4-4-4-4 (in-hold-out-hold) — calming but energizing
- **Wim Hof Style:** 30 deep breaths, hold on exhale, repeat 3 rounds

### 2. Movement
**Get the body activated.** Stagnant body = stagnant mind.

Options:
- Stretching/yoga sequence
- Jumping jacks or bouncing in place
- Dancing
- A short walk outside
- Cold shower (intense but effective)

### 3. Gratitude
**Train the brain to find what's right.** Can't feel fear and gratitude simultaneously.

Three areas:
1. **Past:** Something you're grateful for that happened
2. **Present:** Something you're grateful for right now  
3. **Future:** Something you're anticipating with gratitude (as if it's already happened)

The key: Don't just think it. FEEL it. Bring the emotion into your body.

### 4. Visualization
**See your ideal outcomes vividly.** The brain doesn't fully distinguish between vivid imagination and reality — use this.

Options:
- See yourself succeeding at today's biggest challenge
- Visualize yourself as the person you're becoming
- Run a mental movie of your ideal day
- See past successful moments to anchor confidence

### 5. Intention/Commitment
**Decide who you'll be today.** Set direction before you're swept away.

Options:
- State your "I AM" incantations
- Declare your top priority for the day
- Commit to one specific behavior or standard
- Read your identity statement or vision

## Building a Personalized Ritual

### Step 1: Determine Time Available

Ask: "How much time do you realistically have for a morning practice?"

- **5 minutes:** Abbreviated — breathwork + one gratitude + one intention
- **10 minutes:** Core practice — breathwork + movement + gratitude + quick visualization
- **15-20 minutes:** Full practice — all five elements with depth
- **30+ minutes:** Extended — includes meditation, journaling, physical training

### Step 2: Assess Current State

Ask: "What's your typical morning energy like, and what do you most need?"

- **Low energy, need activation:** Emphasize breathwork, movement, energizing music
- **High anxiety, need grounding:** Emphasize slower breath, gratitude, calming visualization
- **Lack of focus, need direction:** Emphasize intention-setting, visualization of specific outcomes
- **General "blah," need meaning:** Emphasize gratitude, connection to purpose, visualization of who they're becoming

### Step 3: Identify Constraints

Ask:
- "Do you share space with others in the morning?"
- "Can you make noise? Play music? Move around?"
- "What time do you need to be 'online' for work/family?"

Adapt the ritual to be realistic. A perfect ritual you don't do is worthless.

### Step 4: Choose Elements

For each component, select ONE option that fits their time, energy needs, and constraints.

**Principle: Simplicity > Complexity**

Better to do 3 things consistently than design an elaborate 17-step ritual they'll abandon.

### Step 5: Sequence the Ritual

Standard sequence:
1. **Breath** (activate the body, shift physiology)
2. **Movement** (continue activation, get blood flowing)
3. **Gratitude** (shift focus to positive, open the heart)
4. **Visualization** (program the mind for success)
5. **Intention** (lock in commitment for the day)

Can adjust based on preference, but this flow generally works well — body first, then mind, then direction.

## Example Ritual

**User Context:** 15 minutes available, tends toward morning anxiety, needs to feel grounded and focused before work. Lives alone, can make noise.

---

## Your Priming Ritual

**Duration:** 15 minutes
**Best Time:** Immediately after waking, before phone or email
**What You Need:** A chair or cushion, phone for timer (airplane mode)

---

### 1. BREATHWORK [3 min]

**Box Breathing** — calming but energizing.

- Sit comfortably, spine straight
- Inhale through nose for 4 counts
- Hold for 4 counts
- Exhale through mouth for 4 counts
- Hold for 4 counts
- Repeat for 3 minutes (about 9 cycles)

Focus: Feel your body slowing down and grounding.

---

### 2. MOVEMENT [3 min]

**Gentle Activation Sequence:**

- 30 seconds: Neck rolls (slow circles each direction)
- 30 seconds: Shoulder rolls (forward, then back)
- 30 seconds: Spinal twist (seated, looking over each shoulder)
- 30 seconds: Forward fold (standing, let head hang)
- 30 seconds: Arms overhead, stretch side to side
- 30 seconds: Gentle shaking out entire body

Focus: Wake up the body gently, release overnight tension.

---

### 3. GRATITUDE [3 min]

Close your eyes. Bring to mind:

**One moment from your past** you're deeply grateful for. See it. Feel it. Where were you? Who was there? Let the feeling fill your chest.

**One thing in your present life** you're grateful for. It can be simple — your health, your morning coffee, someone who loves you. Feel appreciation, not just think it.

**One thing you're anticipating** in your future, as if it's already happening. See yourself having achieved it. Feel the gratitude now.

*Tip: Put your hands on your heart. It sounds cheesy — it works.*

---

### 4. VISUALIZATION [3 min]

**Today's Ideal Outcome:**

See yourself at the END of today. Everything went well.

- What did you accomplish?
- How did you show up in challenging moments?
- What's the one thing you're most proud of from today?

Now run the movie: See yourself moving through the day with that energy. See yourself calm, focused, effective. See yourself handling the hard thing with grace.

Open your eyes slowly.

---

### 5. INTENTION [3 min]

Say out loud:

*"Today, I choose to be [one quality: calm, focused, present, courageous — pick one]."*

*"My #1 priority today is [specific outcome]."*

*"I am [one "I AM" statement that serves you]."*

Repeat each three times with conviction. Not mumbled — declared.

---

### Getting Started

1. **Do it BEFORE checking your phone.** The phone is reactive energy. Prime first.
2. **Same time, same place, same sequence.** Consistency creates automaticity.
3. **First 7 days are the hardest.** After that, it becomes something you miss when you skip.

### Adaptation Notes

- **Only 5 minutes:** Box breathing (2 min) + One gratitude (1 min) + One intention (2 min)
- **Feeling especially anxious:** Double the breathwork time, use 4-4-6-4 (longer exhale)
- **Feeling flat/unmotivated:** Add 20 jumping jacks before breathwork, use faster music, make visualization more emotionally vivid

---

## Important Notes

- **Non-negotiable > perfect.** A 5-minute practice done daily beats a 30-minute practice done occasionally.
- **Priming is NOT meditation.** Meditation calms. Priming activates and directs. You should feel MORE ready for the day, not sleepier.
- **Adapt to life phases.** When life is chaotic, shorten it. When you have more space, expand it.
- **Track the impact.** Notice how days you prime differ from days you don't. That evidence builds commitment.
- **It's practice, not performance.** Some mornings will feel powerful, others flat. Do it anyway.
