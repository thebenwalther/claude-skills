# Strategic Intervention Skills

Tony Robbins–style breakthrough tools for internal and psychological work. These skills help diagnose and shift emotional states, surface limiting beliefs, resolve values conflicts, and install new patterns and identities.

14 skills across three tiers of intensity:

- **Lightweight / contextual** — fire automatically when the right language appears
- **Structured diagnostics** — run a full framework on a specific problem
- **Deep interventions** — emotionally intensive; explicit triggers only

---

## Skills

### state-story-strategy
Diagnoses whether you're stuck at the **State** (emotional/physiological), **Story** (limiting beliefs), or **Strategy** (missing tactics) level. Most people think they have a Strategy problem when it's actually State or Story.

**Triggers:** `I'm stuck`, `I know what to do but I'm not doing it`, `I feel paralyzed`, `diagnose why I'm stuck`, `SSS diagnostic`, `why can't I execute`

---

### needs-decoder
Identifies which of Tony Robbins' **Six Human Needs** is driving a behavior or conflict — and whether it's being met constructively or destructively.

**Triggers:** `I keep doing X and I don't know why`, `part of me wants X but another part wants Y`, `decode my needs`, `six human needs`, `why do I keep doing this`

---

### values-hierarchy
Uncovers and ranks your core values to reveal why certain decisions feel impossible or why the same internal conflict keeps recurring.

**Triggers:** `what are my values`, `values hierarchy`, `clarify my values`, `why do I feel torn`, `I keep having the same internal conflict`, `help me understand what I actually want`

---

### triad-state-shift
Quick first-aid for a weak emotional state — shifts physiology, focus, and language in 3–5 steps. Fires automatically when you express a low state.

**Triggers:** `I'm stressed`, `I feel overwhelmed`, `I'm anxious`, `I can't focus`, `I'm in a funk`, `I'm unmotivated`

---

### transformational-vocabulary
Swaps disempowering language for empowering alternatives in real-time. Fires inline — no big intervention, just a light reframe.

**Triggers (auto):** `I have to`, `I can't`, `I always fail`, `I'm so stupid`, catastrophizing, harsh self-talk

---

### pattern-interrupt
Suggests physical or mental interrupts to break rumination loops and negative spirals. Fires when you're clearly going in circles.

**Triggers (auto):** repeating the same worry, spiraling, catastrophizing, describing going in circles

---

### leverage-questions
Generates pain/pleasure questions (the Rocking Chair Method) to create emotional urgency for change when you know what to do but haven't done it.

**Triggers:** `I know I need to but...`, `I've been meaning to...`, `I keep putting off...`, `leverage questions`, `rocking chair method`, `help me get motivated`

---

### priming-ritual
Builds a personalized morning priming routine — breath, movement, gratitude, visualization, and intention-setting — in 10–20 minutes.

**Triggers:** `create a priming ritual`, `morning routine`, `priming practice`, `help me start my day better`, `Tony Robbins priming`

---

### rpm-goal-architect
Transforms a vague goal into an RPM plan: **Result** (specific outcome) → **Purpose** (emotional why) → **Massive Action Plan** (prioritized steps).

**Triggers:** `RPM this goal`, `run RPM`, `help me plan [goal]`, `create an action plan for`, `I want to achieve X but don't know where to start`, `break this down for me`

---

### seven-forces-audit
Diagnoses which of Tony Robbins' **7 Forces of Business Mastery** is your current bottleneck and identifies the 2% shift that moves the needle.

**Triggers:** `seven forces`, `7 forces`, `business audit`, `what's holding my business back`, `business bottleneck`, `where should I focus in my business`

---

### modeling-excellence
Reverse-engineers a peak performer — real person, fictional character, or archetype — into a set of beliefs, strategies, and behaviors you can adopt.

**Triggers:** `model X for me`, `how does [person] think`, `what makes [person] successful`, `I want to be more like [person]`, `reverse engineer [person]`

---

### identity-forge
Crafts a new self-concept, `I AM` incantations, and identity statements for who you're becoming. Identity is the strongest lever for behavioral change.

**Triggers:** `help me with my identity`, `who do I want to become`, `I AM statements`, `identity forge`, `create my new identity`, `I want to reinvent myself`, `incantations`

---

### nac-pattern-breaker
Full **Neuro-Associative Conditioning** workflow for breaking an unwanted habit: identify target → get leverage → interrupt → install replacement → condition → future-pace.

**Explicit triggers only:** `run NAC`, `NAC pattern breaker`, `neuro-associative conditioning`, `break this habit`, `help me break the pattern of X`

> This is structured, intensive work. Never auto-triggers.

---

### dickens-protocol
Visualization-based belief-shattering using the *A Christmas Carol* framework — visiting the Past, Present, and Future consequences of a limiting belief to create irreversible leverage for change.

**Explicit triggers only:** `run the Dickens Process`, `Dickens Protocol`, `Ghost of Christmas`, `shatter this belief`, `I need to break this belief`

> Emotionally intense. Requires 20–30 minutes of uninterrupted focus. Never auto-triggers.
