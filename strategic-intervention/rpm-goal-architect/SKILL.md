# RPM Goal Architect

RPM stands for Rapid Planning Method — Tony Robbins' system for turning vague intentions into clear, emotionally-fueled action plans.

Most planning fails because it's:
- Too vague ("get healthy")
- Not emotionally connected (no compelling "why")
- A wish list, not an action sequence

RPM fixes all three.

---

## The RPM Structure

### R — Result
**What specifically do you want?**

The result must be:
- **Specific:** Not "be successful" but "generate $15K/month from my consulting business"
- **Measurable:** You'll know when you've hit it
- **Stated in the positive:** What you want, not what you want to avoid

**Bad results:**
- "Be healthier" (vague)
- "Stop being broke" (negative framing)
- "Be happy" (unmeasurable)

**Good results:**
- "Weigh 175 lbs with visible abs by July 1"
- "Have $50,000 in savings by December 31"
- "Publish my book on Amazon by March 15"

### P — Purpose
**Why do you want this? What will it give you?**

This is the emotional fuel. Without a compelling purpose, you'll quit when it gets hard.

Go at least 3 levels deep on "why":
- "I want to make $15K/month" → Why?
- "So I can quit my job" → Why does that matter?
- "So I can have freedom to work when I want and be present for my kids" → That's the real purpose.

**Purpose must be:**
- Emotionally resonant (not just logical)
- Connected to your values
- Bigger than the goal itself

**Weak purposes:**
- "Because I should"
- "Because everyone else is doing it"
- "To prove people wrong" (this works short-term but burns out)

**Strong purposes:**
- "To prove to myself I'm capable of more"
- "To give my family security I never had growing up"
- "To become the person I know I can be"
- "To make my parents proud"
- "To create something that outlasts me"

### M — Massive Action Plan
**What actions will get you there?**

Not a to-do list. A prioritized sequence of high-leverage actions.

**Rules:**
1. **Brainstorm everything** — don't filter yet
2. **80/20 it** — which 20% of actions will produce 80% of results?
3. **Chunk into categories** — group related actions
4. **Sequence them** — what must come first?
5. **Identify the MIT** — Most Important Thing to do first

## The RPM Process

### Step 1: Capture the Initial Goal

Ask: "What do you want to achieve?"

Listen for vagueness. If they say something vague, ask:
- "What specifically would that look like?"
- "How would you know when you've achieved it?"
- "By when?"

### Step 2: Clarify the Result

Transform their goal into a Result statement:

> "By [specific date], I will [specific measurable outcome]."

**Test it:** Can you know with 100% certainty whether this was achieved? If not, sharpen it.

### Step 3: Uncover the Purpose

Ask "why" repeatedly:
- "Why do you want this?"
- "What will that give you?"
- "And why does THAT matter?"
- "What would achieving this mean about you?"
- "Who else would benefit if you achieved this?"

**Listen for the moment it shifts from logical to emotional.** That's the real purpose.

Have them write a Purpose statement:
> "I'm committed to [Result] because [emotional purpose]. When I achieve this, I will feel [emotions] and it will mean [significance]."

### Step 4: Brainstorm Actions

Ask: "What are ALL the things you could do to achieve this result?"

Don't filter. Capture everything. Quantity over quality at this stage.

Prompt with:
- "What's obvious?"
- "What would someone who's already achieved this tell you to do?"
- "What are you avoiding that you know you should do?"
- "What resources/people/tools do you need?"
- "What skills do you need to develop?"

### Step 5: Prioritize and Chunk

From the brainstorm list:

1. **Star the vital few** — Which 3-5 actions would move the needle most?
2. **Group into categories** — What themes emerge? (e.g., Skills, Relationships, Systems, Daily Habits)
3. **Sequence** — What has to happen first? What depends on what?

### Step 6: Identify the First Massive Action

Ask: "What is the ONE thing you could do in the next 24-48 hours that would create momentum?"

This should be:
- Achievable immediately
- Significant enough to create momentum
- Scary enough to require courage

## Output Format

```
## RPM Plan: [Goal Name]

### The Result
By [date], I will [specific, measurable outcome].

### The Purpose
I'm committed to this because [emotional purpose — at least 3 levels deep].

When I achieve this, I will feel: [emotions]
This will mean: [significance/identity]

### Massive Action Plan

**Category 1: [Theme]**
- [ ] Action 1
- [ ] Action 2
- [ ] Action 3

**Category 2: [Theme]**
- [ ] Action 1
- [ ] Action 2

**Category 3: [Theme]**
- [ ] Action 1
- [ ] Action 2

### The Vital Few (80/20)
1. [Most important action]
2. [Second most important]
3. [Third most important]

### First Massive Action (Do in next 24-48 hours)
[Specific action with clear success criteria]

### Review Schedule
- Daily: [Quick check-in practice]
- Weekly: [Review and adjust]
- Monthly: [Bigger picture assessment]
```

## Example

**User:** "I want to start a podcast"

### The Result
By June 1, I will have published 12 podcast episodes (weekly for 3 months) with at least 100 downloads per episode and 3 guest interviews with people I admire.

### The Purpose
I'm committed to this because I have ideas that could help people, and I've been hiding behind "I'm not ready" for two years. Starting this podcast means I'm finally betting on myself. It's not about becoming famous — it's about becoming the person who ships their work instead of perfecting it forever.

When I achieve this, I will feel: Proud, legitimate, like I finally showed up
This will mean: I'm a creator, not just a consumer. I back myself.

### Massive Action Plan

**Setup & Tech**
- [ ] Choose podcast name (decide by Friday)
- [ ] Buy domain and set up simple website
- [ ] Research and purchase mic ($100 budget)
- [ ] Set up Riverside.fm for recording
- [ ] Choose hosting platform (Buzzsprout or Anchor)
- [ ] Create cover art (Canva or hire on Fiverr)

**Content**
- [ ] Define target listener (1 specific person)
- [ ] Brainstorm 20 episode topics
- [ ] Create episode structure template
- [ ] Write/outline first 4 episodes
- [ ] Record episode 1 (solo)

**Launch**
- [ ] Edit first episode (or hire editor)
- [ ] Write show notes template
- [ ] Submit to Apple, Spotify, Google
- [ ] Announce on social media
- [ ] Tell 10 friends personally

**Guests**
- [ ] Make list of 10 dream guests
- [ ] Draft outreach template
- [ ] Send first 3 guest invitations

### The Vital Few
1. Record and publish Episode 1 (everything else is preparation for this)
2. Define target listener and first 10 topics (clarity on who/what)
3. Send first guest invitation (scary, creates accountability)

### First Massive Action (Next 24-48 hours)
Buy the microphone and schedule a 2-hour block this weekend to record Episode 1. Don't wait until everything is perfect. Ship ugly.

---

## Important Notes

- **Purpose > Result:** If the emotional fuel isn't there, they'll quit. Spend more time on Purpose.
- **Massive means massive:** The action plan should feel slightly overwhelming. Then prioritize ruthlessly.
- **The first action matters most:** Make it concrete, achievable in 24-48 hours, and non-negotiable.
- **Review is part of the plan:** A beautiful RPM that never gets reviewed is just a wish.
- **RPM is recursive:** Big goals can be broken into sub-RPMs. "Launch podcast" might have an RPM just for "Book first guest."
